CrabyTube Flatpak repository

The Flatpak of CrabyTube 0.2.1. Installed from here, CrabyTube gets its updates in your software center, with flatpak update and from its own Settings.

Install it by opening crabytube.flatpakref, or with:

flatpak install --user https://holypandadev.github.io/crabytube-flatpak/crabytube.flatpakref

The commits and the summary are signed with the key in crabytube.gpg.
